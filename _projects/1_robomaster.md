<div class="lang-en-only" markdown="1">
I served as Head of the Mechanical Department for HKUST RoboMaster Team ENTERPRIZE (Sep 2023 – Aug 2025), independently leading the mechanical design of the team's serial-leg infantry, drone, and rudder-wheel infantry gimbal.

Highlights: **RMUC 2024 International Region Champion** and **RoboMaster 2025 University League Champion**.
</div>

<div class="lang-zh-only" markdown="1">

主要荣誉：**RMUC 2024 国际赛区冠军**、**RoboMaster 2025 高校联盟赛冠军**。
