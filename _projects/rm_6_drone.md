<div class="lang-en-only" markdown="1">
The team's aerial support unit — a **13 kg custom quadcopter** that rains fire from above. Its airframe, **2-axis gimbal** payload mount, and projectile-launch hardware carry and precisely dispense a magazine of **~1,600 × 17 mm pellets**. The hard part is the trade-off: enough structure and payload to be a credible threat, yet light and balanced enough to change direction fast while fully loaded.
</div>

<div class="lang-zh-only" markdown="1">
战队的空中支援单元——一台 **13 kg 自研四旋翼**，从空中倾泻火力。机架、**两轴云台**载荷接口与发射结构均为自研，可携带并精确投射 **约 1,600 发 17 mm 弹丸**。难点在于平衡取舍：既要有足够的结构与载荷形成真实威胁，又要在满载时保持轻量与配平，以实现快速机动。
</div>
