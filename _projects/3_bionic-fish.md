<div class="lang-en-only" markdown="1">
**OpenFish 2.0** (ISDN2400, Feb – May 2025): a swordfish-inspired robotic fish with a **1 : 1.2 body-to-tail ratio** drawn from biological data. Our group replaced the original gear drive with a **pull-cord servo mechanism** to eliminate slippage and fishing-line tangling.

The compliant tail uses **six 15 mm-spaced ribs** in graded-perforation 1.8 mm PETG under a 2 mm silicone skin, driven by a NANGU 20 kg servo. Power is dual 2000 mAh LiPo (a single cell couldn't sustain servo current), with **dual-ESP32 + Blynk** control for WiFi-dropout recovery, and a 45° chamfer + O-ring waterproof head.

</div>

<div class="lang-zh-only" markdown="1">
**OpenFish 2.0**（ISDN2400，2025.02 – 05）：一款受剑鱼启发的仿生机器鱼，依据生物数据取 **1 : 1.2 的身尾比例**。我们小组将原始齿轮传动改为**拉线舵机机构**，消除打滑与鱼线缠绕问题。

柔顺尾部采用 **六根 15 mm 间距肋条**，以梯度打孔的 1.8 mm PETG 制成、外覆 2 mm 硅胶，由 NANGU 20 kg 舵机驱动。电源为双 2000 mAh 锂电（单电池无法维持舵机电流），控制采用 **双 ESP32 + Blynk** 以应对 WiFi 掉线，机头为 45° 倒角 + O 形圈防水。

</div>
