<div class="lang-en-only" markdown="1">
Head of Mechanical Department for **HKUST RoboMaster Team ENTERPRIZE** (Nov 2023 – Aug 2025). Independently designed core mechanical systems across the team's robots — serial-leg infantry, drones, and rudder-wheel infantry gimbals (all open-sourced). Awards: **RMUC 2024 International Region Champion** and **RoboMaster 2025 University League Champion**.
</div>

<div class="lang-zh-only" markdown="1">
**HKUST RoboMaster Enterprize 战队**机械部门负责人（2023.11 – 2025.08）。独立完成战队核心机械系统设计——串联腿步兵、无人机、舵轮步兵云台等（已全部开源）。所获荣誉：**RMUC 2024 国际赛区冠军** 与 **RoboMaster 2025 高校联盟赛冠军**。
