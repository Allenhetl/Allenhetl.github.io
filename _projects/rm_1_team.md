Head of Mechanical Department for **HKUST RoboMaster Team ENTERPRIZE** (Sep 2023 – Aug 2025). Independently designed core mechanical systems across the team's robots — serial-leg infantry, drones, and rudder-wheel infantry gimbals (all open-sourced). Awards: **RMUC 2024 International Region Champion** and **RoboMaster 2025 University League Champion**.
