<div class="lang-en-only" markdown="1">
A winter internship co-development project at WeShareTech (Dec 2024 – Jan 2025). The product is an **RFID-based smart reagent cabinet**: chemicals are auto-tracked the moment a tray is removed or replaced, eliminating manual inventory in chemistry labs.

I led the **mechanical structure design**, **hardware component selection**, and **rapid prototyping** for the cabinet enclosure and reader-antenna mounts.

</div>

<div class="lang-zh-only" markdown="1">
WeShareTech 冬季实习联合开发项目（2024.12 – 2025.01）。产品是一款 **RFID 化学试剂智能托盘系统**：试剂托盘取放时即时自动盘点，免去化学实验室人工清点环节。

我负责**机械结构设计**、**硬件选型**与**快速原型制作**，覆盖柜体外壳与读卡天线安装支架。

</div>
