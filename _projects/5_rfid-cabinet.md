<div class="lang-en-only" markdown="1">
A year project (Oct 2024 – Jan 2025). The product is an **RFID-based smart reagent cabinet**: each reagent bottle carries an RFID tag, so the cabinet auto-tracks inventory the moment a bottle is taken out or put back, eliminating manual stocktaking in chemistry labs.

I led the **mechanical structure design**, **hardware component selection**, and **rapid prototyping** for the cabinet enclosure and reader-antenna mounts.

</div>

<div class="lang-zh-only" markdown="1">
学年项目（2024.10 – 2025.01）。作品是一款 **RFID 智能试剂柜**：每瓶试剂贴有 RFID 标签，取放时柜体即时自动盘点，免去化学实验室人工清点环节。

我负责**机械结构设计**、**硬件选型**与**快速原型制作**，覆盖柜体外壳与读卡天线安装支架。
