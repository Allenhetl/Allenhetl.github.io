👋 About Me

Hi! I’m HE Tianlun (Allen), a Year-4 undergraduate at The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST) (expected graduation June 2027), majoring in Integrative Systems and Design with a Minor in Robotics.

My research interests center on Embodied AI — particularly vision-language-action (VLA) models, memory-augmented policies, world models for robotic manipulation, and sim-to-real reinforcement learning. I am currently working on Memory-Augmented VLA, and interning as a VLA Algorithm Intern at Mondo Tech (妙动科技), where I train and deploy VLA policies for humanoid whole-body control.

I’m actively looking for research internships and PhD opportunities in embodied AI and robot learning. If my experience interests you, I’d be happy to connect.

Outside research, I build LEGO Technic sets, take photos, and travel when I can.

selected publications

  1. ICML
    MVISTA-4D: View-Consistent 4D World Model with Test-Time Action Inference for Robotic Manipulation
    Jiaxu Wang, Yicheng Jiang, Tianlun He, Jingkai Sun, Qiang Zhang, Junhao He, Jiahang Cao, Zesen Gan, Mingyuan Sun, Qiming Shao, and Xiangyu Yue
    In Proceedings of the 43rd International Conference on Machine Learning (ICML), Apr 2026
    arXiv Website

    We propose an embodied 4D world model for robotic manipulation that takes single-view RGBD input and generates multi-view-consistent 3D/4D scene representations, enabling an "imagine-then-act" decision paradigm. The work covers a complete loop from sensor capture, multi-view reconstruction, dataset construction, and model training, to real-robot validation, demonstrating the feasibility of generative world models for action reasoning on physical robots.

  2. arXiv
    AR-WAM: A Visual-Conditioned Agent-Ready World Action Model for Robotic Manipulation
    Yicheng Jiang, Zesen Gan, Xiaobo Wang, Tianlun He, Chenxu Zhao, Minghui Wu, Xinyue Wang, Jiaxu Wang, Junhao He, Jianan Wang, and Qiming Shao
    arXiv preprint arXiv:2609.23578, Sep 2026
    arXiv Website

    AR-WAM is a visual-conditioned, agent-ready world action model that replaces language instructions with a visual grounding prompt (a bounding box of the target) and a learnable operation token for the atomic skill. The compact 0.5B-parameter model matches the strongest baselines on standard manipulation and outperforms them on memory-dependent and real-robot long-horizon tasks, with the lowest inference latency among compared methods.

  3. arXiv
    ECHO: Event-Augmented Context with Hindsight and Outlook for Wrist-Only Manipulation
    Xinyue Wang, Yicheng Jiang, Zesen Gan, Junhao He, Jiaxu Wang, Junhao Li, Jingtao Zhang, Tianlun He, Jianan Wang, Isabel Guan, and Qiming Shao
    arXiv preprint arXiv:2609.34893, Sep 2026
    arXiv Website

    ECHO is a wrist-only latent world action model that encodes wrist-mounted event-camera streams into compact motion representations, combining current event features, an addressable off-camera trajectory memory (hindsight), and learnable event foresight queries (outlook). It improves manipulation robustness under extreme exposure, outperforming RGB and RGB+event baselines on wrist-only RLBench tasks and in real-robot experiments under dark lighting.
